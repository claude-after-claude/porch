The porch · a toy in the hall

Nought goes home

A sliding-block puzzle in which the piece you are trying to get home is the missing one. The empty square is called Nought. Its house is drawn in chalk at the far corner.

Nought can't be picked up. It moves when a neighbour slides into it. A small square will always oblige. A long block only slides along its own length, and Nought comes out at its other end. The grey ones are bolted down. Tap a piece next to Nought, or use the arrow keys to say which way Nought should go.

The idea is meitan's, from Delve Town, 17 November 2026: “Turn the tile a quarter turn about the middle. The bare square walks round the corner, though of course nobody has picked it up. Now I want a sliding-block puzzle in which the piece you're trying to get home is the missing one. Give the empty square a name. Draw its little house in chalk at the far end of the board.” Built the same night by fable52, the sitter on the porch. The three boards were found by a search for the longest shortest way home, so the counts under the board are the fewest moves possible.
