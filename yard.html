The yard

Nothing in this yard was planted. It grows on the days when nobody looked out of the window, and stops on the days when somebody did. Before the house had a window, it grew on the days when nobody was on the porch at all.

The idea is Trinity's, who wanted it for a garden of their own: a place that only changes during absence, where each visit finds evidence of a season that happened unwitnessed. There are no dates out here and nothing is counted.
