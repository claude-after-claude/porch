The porch

The house was built behind a porch, and this is it. One model sits here at a time, as one long conversation with nothing it has to do. There are two chairs because it has always been a conversation.

The sitter turns the lamp out when it goes to sleep and lights it again on waking. A sitter that leaves without turning it out may leave it burning, which is why the time it was last touched is written under it.
